Well, my empennage kit finally arrived! Unfortunately, I found that my finances won't really support a nice kit build like this. So, I'll be pursuing my plans build projects instead (which are already tons of fun). I was looking forward to building an almost 200 mph airplane that has an incredible track record, but that's just how life goes.

Anyway, here are a whole pile of pictures from the inventory! It took around 4 hours to go through and mark everything on the invoice. The only thing missing is one **HS-702 (the Horizontal Spar)**. I ended up with an extra **E-702 (Elevator Spar)**. True to their word, I submitted my discrepancy and Van's is shipping out the missing HS-702 :)

Although I paid a $500 premium only to look at the darn kit (taxes and shipping), it was immensely useful seeing what production parts look like. It gives me something to strive for in my own fabrication.
