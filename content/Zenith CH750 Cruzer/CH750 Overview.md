This is a blog that's documenting a Zenith CH750 Cruzer build!

# Mission Profile
Originally, this was meant to be the inexpensive cross-country tourer. However, I decided that if I want a true cross-country machine - that wouldn't take me forever to travel the US with - then I should pursue a kit instead.

I still have the plans and intend to build the Zenith though! Especially since I found I really enjoy fabricating as well as assembly. Instead, it'll take on a more specialized role. At this time, I'm exploring the possibility of making it a float plane :)
