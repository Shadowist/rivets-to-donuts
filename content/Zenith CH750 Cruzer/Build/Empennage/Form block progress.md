Slowly making progress on the first form block for the rudder! At least after gather a bunch more tools to make my life easier.

My process so far:

1. Use circular saw to do the rough cut.

2. Then, use a hacksaw to get closer to my lines.

3. Then, use the bench sander to really grind it down.

The next step will be to use a router to set up the proper radius.

While this works okay, I need to see if I can use a wood cutting router bit or something to really shave off wood faster. The current workflow is just a bit too slow. Otherwise, I’ll have to invest in a band saw.
