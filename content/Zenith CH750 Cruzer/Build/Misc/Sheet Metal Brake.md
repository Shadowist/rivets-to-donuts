All in, we're looking at $3400 of fabricated parts if ordered from Zenith (not including shipping). The total sheet metal cost I had estimated to be $5-6k roughly. I imagine this could cut into that somewhat, particularly since I'd have to order large sheets for the skins.

Now, I *could* buy an entry-level 8' automotive sheet metal brake for about $2k. For less than $1k, I might be able to build my own as well. The question is: how much would I really save with each possible avenue? With such large pieces, how much would it cost if I botch the fabrication and have to order a new sheet?!
